Your base camp on the Oaxacan coast

Bungalows El Maguey is a small, owner-run hotel in Colonia Santa María, just around the corner from Zicatela's main strip. Wake up to the garden, cool off in the pool or the cold plunge, train in the rooftop gym, and finish the day in a hammock under the palapa watching the waves roll in.

Christian lives in town and is on hand for anything you need — from surf tips to scooter rentals to the best tacos nearby.

The bungalows

Six private studio apartments

Each bungalow is fully self-contained with its own kitchen, bathroom and balcony overlooking the garden. Units 1–3 are on the ground floor; units 4–6 are upstairs.

The property
